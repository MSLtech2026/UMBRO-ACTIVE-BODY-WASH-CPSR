Executive Overview
One-Page Safety Summary
OVERALL CONCLUSION: SAFE FOR INTENDED USE
Umbro Inspire Body Wash is a rinse-off cleansing gel assessed under Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 and the SCCS Notes of Guidance (11th Rev., SCCS/1628/21). All ingredients have a Margin of Safety (MoS) >100. The product has passed accelerated stability testing, preservation challenge testing (ISO 11930), and the fragrance is IFRA 51st Amendment compliant. No substances from Annexes II, III (beyond permitted limits) or IV of the EU Cosmetics Regulation are used at unsafe levels.

Non-Animal Testing Statement

In accordance with Article 18 of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, no animal testing has been conducted on this cosmetic product or any of its ingredients for the purposes of developing this product. All safety data referenced in this report has been sourced from:

  • Published peer-reviewed scientific literature
  • CIR (Cosmetic Ingredient Review) Expert Panel safety assessments
  • SCCS (Scientific Committee on Consumer Safety) opinions
  • RIFM (Research Institute for Fragrance Materials) fragrance safety data
  • Supplier-provided technical and safety data sheets (SDS/TDS)
  • In vitro and human patch test data where applicable
The preservation challenge test (ISO 11930 modified repeat-challenge) and accelerated stability study were conducted using non-animal methodologies. No in vivo animal studies were commissioned for this product.
